Import a team from ESPN

Paste your league link and pick your team. We'll copy your roster in. Run it again anytime to update it.

1. Paste your league link

On ESPN, open your league (or your team page) and copy the address from your browser's address bar.

Private league? Most ESPN leagues are.

Easiest: ask your league manager to turn on League → Settings → Basic Settings → "Make League Viewable to Public". After that, the link alone works.

Or paste two cookies from your browser while signed in to ESPN:

  1. Open fantasy.espn.com in Chrome or Firefox while signed in.
  2. Press F12 → Application (Chrome) or Storage (Firefox) → Cookies → https://fantasy.espn.com.
  3. Copy the values of espn_s2 and SWID below.

These are used only to read your league for this import. We don't save them.
